04 — Case Studies

Fraud-prevention and process-improvement work recognised by the client and featured in the company newsletter — part of 30+ process-improvement suggestions submitted, several of which have gone into implementation for the client and Flipkart.

Grocery Pricing Fraud — Revenue Recovered Fraud Prevention

Flagged a pattern on Flipkart Grocery where products were being manipulated in listing/pricing to be received far below their real value — cases ranging from items priced ₹100–150 being used to receive goods worth ₹4,000–5,000. The client investigated, confirmed the modus operandi and individual involved, recovered the leaked revenue, and initiated legal action. Total leakage identified: ₹1,07,393 across 162 units, including a single item worth ₹1,18,994 sold for just ₹11,601.

AI-Generated Image Refund Fraud Preventive Fix

As fake-refund claims using AI-generated product photos kept rising and became a growing concern for Flipkart, proposed restricting refund proof to live camera capture only — removing the gallery-upload option so AI-generated images can no longer be submitted. The client acknowledged the suggestion and shared it forward for implementation.

Order Cancellations — Re-ship/Re-order Fix Customer Experience

Studied a pattern of orders getting cancelled without customers being fully aware, leaving advisors to manage the resulting escalations. Proposed a Re-ship / Re-order option so customers get more control instead of a plain cancellation — reducing escalations and improving overall satisfaction. Acknowledged by the client and forwarded to the product team.

+ Case study: identified a Proof-of-Delivery (POD) gap in grocery orders — delivery agents would photograph the full basket for POD but hold back the highest-value item (e.g. ghee) before handover, leaving agents unable to approve genuine return requests since the POD photo showed the item as delivered. Documented and highlighted for the client.
